# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Providing well-sourced, verifiable contributions
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism and correction of stale data
- Focusing on what is best for the community of users relying on this data

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks
- Submitting deliberately false pricing data or astroturfing on behalf of a vendor
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening an issue or contacting the maintainer via the DarkDataLabs website. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and may ban contributors for behaviors deemed inappropriate.
